# impeccable/multiplane-cel.html

Scope: one-page hiring site for Ines Rook, Persuade mode. Audience: festival programmers, bakery owners, a product lead comparing designers. Action: email hello@inesrook.studio. Proof: five synthetic projects, the four-step process. No scroll animation and no parallax (user): the multiplane composition is frozen, the depth carried by painting and focus. Static HTML/CSS, a few lines of JS for rack focus only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Five painted planes pretending to be one morning: identity, screens, illustration and print are separate planes shot by one camera, and the lit cottage window is the one person working. Refuses the thumbnail grid and the scroll-parallax cliche.

OWN-WORLD: Deep umber ground, gouache forest greens, mist blue-grey ridges, a warm dawn sky, the cottage lamp amber as the single active accent. Text always sits on the nearest cel. Gloock title-card serif over Alegreya Sans.

STORY: The visitor arrives inside a painted dawn with a lit window, reads five painted scenes, racks focus through the four planes to learn the disciplines, reads the process as exposures, sees the one lamp, emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed painted dawn: far ridges, mid forest, cottage with lit window at right, near ferns along the foot. Head in cream serif on the dark forest at left over columns 1 to 6, one sentence, amber-lit email button.

FORM: Multiplane Cel Dawn (challenger, catalog cinema-cinematography-editing-multiplane-cel-dawn), adopted by the user; seed:c2fcc778f7fd6f94. Signature interaction: rack focus: choosing a discipline sharpens its plane of the scene and softens the others.
