# impeccable/identity-manual.html

Scope: one-page hiring site for Ines Rook, Persuade mode. Audience: festival programmers, bakery owners, a product lead comparing designers. Action: email hello@inesrook.studio. Proof: five synthetic projects, the four-step process. No scroll animation (user). Static HTML/CSS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Ines's own identity manual: one language specified across every touchpoint, the way Dutch identity manuals of the 1960s and 70s specified a railway or a post office. Refuses the thumbnail grid.

OWN-WORLD: Off-white manual stock on a grey binder board, ring-binding holes down the left of every sheet, coloured index tabs down the right edge. Black ink, one signal red and one blue, each swatch printed with its codes. Familjen Grotesk display over Public Sans; every section carries a manual number.

STORY: The visitor opens the manual, reads each project as a spec page (artwork beside its typefaces, colours and formats), sees the applications and the procedure, then the contact specification.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The cover sheet. A red square block at top left, the head flush left under it across eight columns, one sentence and the email button, edition line at the foot. Index tabs 0 to 5 run down the right edge.

FORM: My own top grounded candidate, the Identity Manual (picked by the user as an additional build); seed:c2fcc778f7fd6f94. Signature interaction: the right-edge index tabs, always present, mark the section being read.
