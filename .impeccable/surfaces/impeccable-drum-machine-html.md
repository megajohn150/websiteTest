Scope: one-page hiring site for Ines Rook, Persuade mode. Audience: festival programmers, bakery owners, a product lead comparing designers. Action: email hello@inesrook.studio. Proof: five synthetic projects, the four-step process. No scroll animation and no running chase light (user): state changes only. Static HTML/CSS, a few lines of JS for the pattern banks and step keys.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio as one rhythm machine: sixteen steps quartered into four disciplines, and every project is a pattern programmed on the same row by the same hands. Refuses the thumbnail grid.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal panel ground with screw heads, a step row of red, orange, yellow and white keys with LEDs above, red segmented readouts, silkscreen-white labels. Chakra Petch for panel lettering, Share Tech Mono only inside readouts, Archivo for reading.

STORY: The visitor punches pattern banks 1 to 5 and watches each project light its disciplines on the row, reads the five patterns, the four bars of the process, the readout of capacity and booking date, emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: head in wide capitals, one sentence, red email key. Right: the machine panel with year and pattern readouts, five pattern-bank keys, and the sixteen-step row quartered and labelled, lit for pattern 1.

FORM: Drum Machine Step Row (challenger, catalog signals-instruments-drum-machine-step-row), adopted by the user; seed:c2fcc778f7fd6f94. Signature interaction: the pattern banks: each selects a project and lights the steps of its disciplines; each step key arms and accents on its own.
