## Direction contract

THESIS: One sheet, one pair of hands: the whole job is one square of paper folded by one person into a finished form. Refuses the portfolio grid of thumbnails under a big name.

OWN-WORLD: Vermilion washi ground with kozo fibre and fold-white crease lines; washi-cream sheets for reading; sumi-black notes; one gold dot marks the current step. Jost light display over Source Sans 3; every number lives in a strict margin column.

STORY: A visitor sees one sheet become a crane, reads the five finished pieces, folds through the four steps, learns it is one person, emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Vermilion field. Head at left over columns 1 to 5, one sentence and the email button under it. A crease-pattern square on columns 6 to 10 with the paper crane standing on it. The margin column at right lists the five projects 01 to 05, gold dot on 01.
