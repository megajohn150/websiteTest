How a project runs in 4 steps

  1. Step 1: a call and a fixed quote

    We talk through what you are making and when it has to exist. You get a brief back and one price.

  2. Step 2: three sketches on paper

    Three different answers to the brief, drawn by hand first. You pick one to build.

  3. Step 3: the system on real things

    The chosen direction is tested on the bag, the screen and the poster before anything is final.

  4. Step 4: files and a walkthrough

    Print files, Figma files and guidelines, then an hour together so your team can use them.
