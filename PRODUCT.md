# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS, no build step (chosen earlier in this project; every page is a standalone .html file with its own stylesheet folder under styles/).

## Users

Prospective clients of Ines Rook, a fictional independent graphic designer in Rotterdam. A mix, matching the portfolio: cultural organisations (festivals, museums, record labels), small local brands (bakeries, shops), and the occasional digital product team (a banking app). They arrive deciding whether to hire her and want to see the work, understand what she does and how a project runs, then make contact.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio and hiring site. Success is a visitor who has seen the work, understands the four disciplines, and emails hello@inesrook.studio about a project.

## Positioning

One person covers the whole job: identity, screens, illustration and posters, so everything a client ships speaks one visual language. No account managers, no hand-offs; four projects at a time.

## Operating Context

Visitors are commissioners comparing designers, usually on a laptop at work, sometimes on a phone from a shared link. Hiring happens by email, not a form.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Disciplines: brand identity; digital and UI (handed over in Figma); illustration; posters and print.
- Process: Listen (a call, a brief, a fixed quote), Sketch (three directions, one chosen), Build (the system tested on real things), Hand over (files, guidelines, a walkthrough).
- Booking projects from February 2027. Based in Rotterdam, Netherlands.
- Contact: hello@inesrook.studio. Social: Instagram, Are.na, LinkedIn (placeholder links).

## Evidence on Hand

All content is synthetic demonstration material requested by the user; the person and projects are fictional:
- Tidewater Festival, poster series, 2025 (twelve posters, one per night, two colours; Rotterdam, 14 to 17 August).
- Hallo Bakery, identity and packaging, 2025 ("bread and coffee").
- Ferro, banking app interface, 2024.
- Small Hours Records, sleeves and illustration for a late-night jazz label, 2024.
- Museum of Maps, wayfinding and identity, 2023 (Room 4, Cartography).

Absent and not to be invented: testimonials, client logos, project counts, metrics, prices, awards, press.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; copy stays short and factual.
2. One designer, one language: the page itself should feel authored by the person it sells.
3. Hiring is one plain action: an email.
4. Never claim what was not given.
